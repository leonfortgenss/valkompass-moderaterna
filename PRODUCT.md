# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Voters in Stenungsunds kommun of all ages and political backgrounds. They are deciding how to vote in the 2026 municipal election and want an honest, easy way to see which local party aligns with their views. Many are not politically engaged day-to-day — the tool must work without prior knowledge of the parties or issues.

## Product Purpose

"Vilket Stenungsund vill du ha?" — a satirical political test built and branded by Moderaterna i Stenungsund. Voters answer 25 local questions (skola, ekonomi, Campus Nösnäs, trygghet, äldreomsorg, samhällsplanering, klimat/industri/beredskap, politiskt ledarskap), picking from four options per question: one reflects Moderaterna's actual position, the other three are satirical exaggerations of other political reflexes (never naming other parties). The result shows a match percentage with Moderaterna's politics, a satirical "profile" (e.g. "Den handlingskraftiga framtidsbyggaren"), three sub-scores (Handlingskraft, Ekonomiskt ansvar, Framtidstro), and a handful of questions where the user agreed/disagreed with Moderaterna — ending with a clear, respectful reveal of the sender. Success means the test is shared widely, feels funny and fair rather than mean-spirited, and is something a Moderaterna representative could stand behind publicly.

## Brand Personality

Modern, enkel, tillgänglig — the interface should feel approachable and clear, not intimidating or political. Calm confidence, not campaign energy.

## Anti-references

- **Flashig valreklam** — no loud colors, bold slogans, or campaign-poster energy
- **Generisk myndighetssite** — not sterile or dated; it should feel current without being trendy
- **Tech-startup aesthetic** — no SaaS gradients, dark modes, or "product launch" energy; this is a civic tool
- **Partibroschyr** — should not feel like pure Moderaterna marketing; neutrality builds trust even when the brand is present

## Design Principles

1. **Neutrality builds trust** — Moderaterna branding is present but restrained; the tool must feel fair to voters of all political views
2. **Clarity over cleverness** — every question, answer option, and result must be immediately understandable for all ages and literacy levels
3. **Local before generic** — references to Stenungsund, local issues, and Swedish political culture; not a template that could be any town
4. **Accessible by default** — WCAG AA minimum; legible typography, sufficient contrast, keyboard navigable, no motion required to use

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Large enough touch targets for older users on mobile. Sufficient color contrast on all text. No information conveyed by color alone. Respects prefers-reduced-motion.
